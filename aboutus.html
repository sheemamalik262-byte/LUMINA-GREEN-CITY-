About Lumina Green City

Pakistan's next-generation residential housing scheme — designed to deliver luxury, community, and modern living in one thoughtfully planned destination.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To build Pakistan's most resident-centric housing scheme — where every family finds a safe, beautiful, and well-connected home equipped with world-class amenities, sustainable infrastructure, and a thriving community spirit.

Our Vision

To transform urban living in Pakistan by creating landmark communities that blend green environments with modern infrastructure — setting a new benchmark for residential development in the country.

Our Core Values
Principles that guide everything we build
Quality Construction
Every brick, road, and structure is built to last. We use certified materials and experienced contractors with no compromise on quality.
Green Living
Parks, green belts, and tree-lined streets are part of our blueprint. We believe every resident deserves access to nature.
Safety First
24/7 security, gated access, CCTV across all sectors, and a trained security force ensure your family is always protected.
Transparency
All pricing, availability and documentation is clear and accessible. No hidden charges. No surprises. Just honest dealing.
Community Faith
Mosques, schools, and parks ensure a spiritually and socially connected community for residents of all ages.
Modern Infrastructure
Uninterrupted electricity, gas, water, wide roads and internet-ready infrastructure for a 21st century lifestyle.
